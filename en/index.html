The house

A real country house, a short walk from the finest sea in the Marche

At Trave, in the hamlet of Montacuto, the countryside still runs all the way to the cliffs. From here you are in the water at Mezzavalle or Portonovo in five minutes, and in the centre of Ancona in ten.

The house is a restored farmhouse with beamed ceilings, terracotta floors and a large living room with a fireplace. Outside there is a fenced garden looking onto Monte Conero, with a glimpse of the sea between the palms. It is the right place for a family or a group of friends who want space, quiet and the beach close by.

Around the house

The Conero beaches on foot, Ancona in ten minutes

The house sits inside the Conero Regional Park, on the northern side of the mountain. The wildest beaches on the coast are a walk away, the equipped ones a few minutes by car.

The Trave rocks600 m

A long natural reef running into the sea: snorkelling, sunsets and very few people.

Mezzavalle beach1.1 km

A free beach at the foot of the cliffs, one of the most beautiful on the Conero. Reached by footpath.

Portonovo5 km

The bay with its Romanesque church, seafood restaurants and local mussels, beach clubs and the Napoleonic fort.

Ancona6.6 km from the station

The old town, the harbour, the Passetto and the cathedral of San Ciriaco. Trains, and ferries to Croatia.

Sirolo and Numana20 min

The villages on the sea, the Due Sorelle rocks by boat, walks in the Conero park.

Ancona airport19 km

Falconara Marittima, 25 minutes by car. A14 motorway exit Ancona Sud, 15 minutes away.

Frequently asked questions

How many people can the house sleep?

Up to 6 guests: one double bedroom, one twin bedroom and a sofa bed in the living room. The house is entirely yours, there are no other guests.

How far is the sea?

The Trave rocks are 600 metres away and Mezzavalle beach about 1.1 km, reachable on foot by the path. Portonovo is 5 km, a few minutes by car.

Are pets allowed?

Yes, dogs and cats are welcome at no extra charge. The garden is fenced.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in from 15:00, check-out by 10:00. Please let us know your arrival time in advance so we can welcome you in person.

Is there parking?

Yes, free gated private parking inside the property, no reservation needed.

Is it cheaper to book direct?

Yes. Booking through the site, on WhatsApp or by phone means no platform commission: the direct rate is the lowest we offer.

How do I get there?

By car: A14 exit Ancona Sud, then towards Ancona and Montacuto (15 minutes). By train: Ancona station, 6.6 km. By plane: Ancona Falconara airport, 19 km. A car is recommended to get around the coast.
