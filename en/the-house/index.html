The farmhouse

All the space of a house, not just a room

A restored farmhouse with beamed ceilings, terracotta floors and a large living room with a fireplace.

Outside there is a fenced garden looking onto Monte Conero, with a glimpse of the sea between the palms. Private entrance and private parking: the right place for a family or a group of friends who want space, quiet and the beach close by.

Double bedroom
